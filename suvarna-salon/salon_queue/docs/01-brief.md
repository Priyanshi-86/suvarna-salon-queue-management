# Salon Queue Management System

## Problem
When wait times are unknown and paper lists get messy, walk-outs become an invisible loss for salon. The software gives didgital waiting list and booking appoinments.

## Users
Client, Owner/receptionist, Stylist.

## Must-have features
1. Client can do Online booking via book appoinment using their phone/ Laptop to choose service with Stylist.Easy scheduling and cancellation. Instant alert for both client and Stylist.
2. Client: Live status(estimate waiting time, queue position) in dashboard,my bookings, previous bookings, queue tracking.  
3. Live status: Alert 'Hey, you're the next!'.
4. Receptionist Dashboard: checks and verifies remote clients and add walk-ins, approves to go for service and update the status of that client and Stylist after the completion of the service.
5. Stylist screen: schedule wise client, update the status to start and eventually to done.
6. Owner Dashboard: Overall client flow on that day/that month with schedules, todays appoinments, cancellations. Stylists overall graph with attended clients.
7. Waiting room TV: The list of clients with their schedules with thier Stylist. To verify and staty updated for their turn.

## Dont's build
Online Payment system, Inventory Management, Whatsapp Marketing, CRM, Feedback, staff management, E-commerce, POS Billing Siftware, multiple branches.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: Warm ivory, soft sand, muted taupe, charcoal; brushed-gold accent. Elegant serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for UI text. Clean, mobile first.
Labels in english, hindi and gujrati.
